# Prior work (checked 26 November 2026)

No full recalculation of the CDG gamma-ray bound with current cosmology and data
was found. This is not proof that none exists.

* Cohen, De Rújula, Glashow, astro-ph/9707087 – the original bound.
* Baur, Blanchard, von Ballmoos, arXiv:1512.08482 – Planck 2015 parameters, but
  a different (Gaussian density field) model, scaling arguments rather than a
  recalculation, COMPTEL comparison. Reports a much worse CMB fit for that model.
* von Ballmoos, arXiv:1401.7258 – review arguing the question deserves a new look.
* Dolgov, hep-ph/0211260 – review, including problems with early separation
  mechanisms.

## How the search was done, and its limits

Web searches plus the INSPIRE list of 389 papers citing CDG. 97 were selected by
title words and screened by title only. The other 292 were not screened, and
work that does not cite CDG or is not indexed could be missed.
